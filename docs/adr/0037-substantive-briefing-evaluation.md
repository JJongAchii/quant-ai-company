# 0037 — Substantive briefs and actual-output evaluation

Status: implemented and evaluated on saved originals; live acceptance remains separate.

The owner rejected thin examples and asked to verify professional content quality before relying on
daily delivery. A role title, analytical schema, fixture score or long message cannot establish that quality.
This extends ADR 0036 with publication format 4 and an actual-output evaluation record.

Keep the existing Analyst identity, schedule, registered sources, subscription authentication and durable
execution. Sample closing reports and distinct market, policy, geopolitical, corporate and cross-asset
topics before filling a bounded 16-article context. Collection keywords are a retrieval heuristic, not proof
that all material events were found. Record missing topics and require semantic source-by-source review.

The main message contains orientation, a market overview, dated prices, up to six substantial issues and
near-term checkpoints. All material issues stay in the main message. Essential contradictory evidence and
quantitative context must be in sourced published prose, not only raw evidence or internal diagnostics.
Keep detailed economic mechanisms and source timestamps in the thread. Length is diagnostic, not a score.

Add coverage, depth and readability to the nine analytical/factual checks. The reviewer sees the actual
main-message preview and accounts for every supplied original, with cited item IDs or an exclusion reason.
Incomplete or rejected content cannot qualify as a full brief. Unsupported central conclusions are withheld.
The same-model second call is an editorial check, not an independent human or market-data authority.

After a confirmed failed first review, permit at most one correction and final review if more than fifteen
minutes remain before the deadline. Reuse the frozen originals; preserve every request and response.
Compact only repeated quotations in feedback, not original evidence. Respect existing limits and the news
model lane. Never treat an uncertain response as permission for a replacement inference. Extend the durable
phase constraint to revise/final_review and require their actual receipts when qualifying corrected editions.
If only observable conditions fail and every other editorial check passes, allow a typed patch for exactly
those condition IDs. Preserve every other claim and observation, validate the merged proposal and perform
the same final full review. This stays inside the single correction allowance and avoids regenerating sound
facts to repair an isolated weak checkpoint. Render every supporting source reference, not only the first two.

Numerical checks normalize exact written units, dates and simple English counts, and distinguish quantity
from alphanumeric entity names. They do not establish semantics or causality. Literal whitespace inside a
nested JSON quotation may be decoded losslessly; arbitrary syntax repair and quotation rewriting are excluded.

Assess saved real AM/PM source snapshots using actual subscription inference, deterministic validation,
separate editorial calls and a different subscription model blinded to author/version. Preserve failed
attempts, challenge cases, inputs and readable results. Corrections on these same examples are development,
not held-out performance. The results report separates this work from human expert calibration, five future
trading days, current-lake integration and actual Slack publication.
