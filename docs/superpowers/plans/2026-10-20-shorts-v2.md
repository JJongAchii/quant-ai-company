# Daily Shorts v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the text-only Shorts (hook, 3 numbers, outro) with a news-style vertical cut. It opens with a fixed date line, shows the day's issues as a list, then explains each issue over the episode's own photos, and ends with the signals to watch and the fixed outro line.

**Architecture:** The model still writes one typed plan, `ShortsPlan`, under the unchanged contract name `video_shorts_v1`. The service adds:
- the fixed lines: date, agenda, outro;
- each issue's picture, taken from the episode scenes the issue cites;
- the timing.

`ShortsRenderer` renders the new `shorts_design/` template frame by frame on the worker, as today. The new step is that it mixes an optional music bed under the narration.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, pydantic, Playwright Chromium, FFmpeg, PostgreSQL. The tests use `uv run pytest`, real PostgreSQL, and real Chromium/FFmpeg for render tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-shorts-v2-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Do not touch render inputs: `src/quant_company/contracts.py`, any module `video/remote.py` imports (episode.py, motion.py, render.py, illustrations.py, …), and `video/design/`. `shorts.py`, `shorts_store.py`, `runner.py`, `shorts_design/` and `providers/claude_runner.py` are not render inputs.
  - Check with `deploy/release.py` `render_changes(paths, [repo_root])`. It must return `[]`.
- The contract name stays `video_shorts_v1`. Only the `ShortsPlan` schema in `video/shorts.py` changes, and claude_runner imports it.
- Frame size: 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Every text box stays inside `SAFE = {'left': 70, 'right': 940, 'top': 230, 'bottom': 1500}`.
- Fixed lines (exact copy):
  - Date line: `f"{M}월 {D}일 {아침|마감} 브리핑입니다."`. Use `아침` for edition_kind `am` and `마감` for `close` (existing `EDITION_WORDS`).
  - Agenda line: `f"오늘 볼 이슈는 {KO_COUNT[n]} 가지입니다."` with `KO_COUNT = {2: '두', 3: '세', 4: '네', 5: '다섯'}`. That gives `오늘 볼 이슈는 다섯 가지입니다.`.
  - Outro line: `OUTRO['say']`, unchanged (`왜 그런지, 앞으로 볼 신호는 본편에서 확인하세요.`).
- Issues: 2–5 (aim 4–5).
  - Each issue cites 1–4 episode scene IDs, all from chapters `핵심 이슈` or `함께 볼 이슈`.
  - The first cited scenes of successive issues follow episode order, and no scene is cited by two issues.
- Length: aim 60–75 s. Hard limits 20–100 s. Outside 45–90 s is a finding, not a failure.
- Every number in the cut must appear in the scene's cited briefing lines (as v1 already checks), and also in the delivered episode plan text. `CHECKS` becomes 3.
- No on-screen AI label, no '보도에 따르면', no buy/sell wording, no tool wording, no series name (`|`, `증시story`) in the title.
- Real photos (`kind == '자료사진'`) carry the credit chip `자료사진 · {author} · {license}` inside the safe area, and a credit line in the upload text. Generated pictures carry nothing.
- Music: optional. Use `{settings.video_asset_dir}/music/*.mp3`, sorted, and pick index = `date.toordinal() % count`.
  - The bed sits at about −22 dB under the narration with sidechain ducking, and fades in/out over 1 s. With no file there is no music, and the manifest gets `"music": null`.
- Production rules stay as they are: QUIET windows, episode first, credit reserve 80, 3 writes, the recheck on a `CHECKS` change (#191), and the release busy hold (#187).

## Review Focus
1. An episode whose issue chapters hold only 2 usable scenes. The cut is made with 2 issues and is not skipped. Test in Task 1 (`test_two_issues_is_enough`).
2. An issue whose cited scenes have no picture. It falls back to the episode `thumbnail_image`, and failing that to the first issue picture, shown blurred. It never shows a bare navy frame. Test in Task 3 (`test_every_scene_has_a_picture`).
3. A v1 receipt meeting the v2 recheck. The plan fails `ShortsPlan` validation, so it is skipped without crashing the tick, and the cut ends `skipped/check_failed` with `qa.checks == 3`. Test in Task 4 (`test_recheck_skips_versions_of_the_old_shape`).
4. A missing or unreadable music folder. The render goes on without music. Test in Task 3 (`test_music_is_optional`).
5. A long Korean issue body or label. It shrinks (`data-fit`) or wraps inside the safe area, and the layout QA reports overflow as a finding. Test in Task 3 (the real render asserts `manifest['findings']` has no `outside-safe-area` for the fixture).

  - `ShortsRenderer.render(job, plan, episode: dict, directory, audio_paths: list[Path], stop=None) -> dict`. The manifest gains `'music': str | None`, `'pictures': [{'scene': id, 'file': name, 'credit': str}]`, and the scene list `opener, agenda, <issue ids>, outro`.
  - The page `EP` gets these scenes: `{'id','type': 'opener'|'agenda'|'issue'|'outro','data','t0','t1','bg': 'img/<file>'|null,'blur': bool,'credit': str}`, plus `label` (e.g. `10.20 아침`) and `date_badge` (`10월 20일 아침`).

Layout follows the approved storyboard `docs/superpowers/specs/assets/2026-10-20-shorts-v2-storyboard.jpg`:
- **Background:** a full-bleed photo with Ken Burns (scale 1.00→1.08 across the scene) and a dark gradient.
- **Header:** logo + 뭐든story at the top-left of the safe area, the gold date badge at the top-right, and the segmented progress bar (one segment per scene).
- **Opener:** kicker `오늘의 질문`, the headline (numbers gold), and the question.
- **Agenda:** kicker `오늘 볼 이슈 N가지` and numbered rows `label` / `agenda`. The rows appear one by one over the first ~60% of the scene.
- **Issue:** pill `k  label  k/N`, the big number with ▲/▼ only from `dir`, `big_label`, a body card, a note tag `note_kind · note`, and the credit chip when there is one.
- **Outro:** kicker `확인할 신호`, the signal rows, and a red `▶ 자세한 해석은 본편에서` button.
- **Captions:** a box at the bottom inside the safe area (y ≈ 1390–1500), as in v1.

Timing:
- Each scene lasts `LEAD + audio + TAIL`.
- The agenda lasts at least `1.0 + 0.9 × n` s, and the outro at least 6.0 s. While a scene is held, its audio is padded with silence.

Music:
- Pick the track per the Global Constraints.
- Mix: `[1:a]volume=0.08,afade=t=in:d=1,afade=t=out:st={total-1}:d=1[m];[m][0:a]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.03:ratio=8:attack=20:release=400[d];[0:a][d]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:normalize=0`, applied after narration loudnorm.
